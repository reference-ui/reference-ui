## 6. Approval request (flags for the captain)

The mission scope enumerated `types.rs`, `assembly.rs`, `runtime/builder.rs`, `lib.rs`, and
the 11 stations, but three more files inside `packages/reference-rs/modules/atomic/**` were
necessary to reach a green, compiling tree, and one of them is a harness semantic change:

1. `native.rs` — required (crate won't compile otherwise). Mirrors §F's field.
2. `js/types.ts` — the T01 `external` marker was absent from the hand-maintained TS mirror;
   `tests/helpers.ts` needs it.

   any external want exists. The gauge now keeps the strict coverage identity for external-free
   stations and checks emitted-class injectivity when external wants are present. This is a
   shared-harness change affecting all stations uniformly; no committed baseline moved.

If the captain prefers a different reconciliation (e.g. a dedicated external-count field or a
different gauge split), the engine changes stand on their own and only the gauge needs revising.
