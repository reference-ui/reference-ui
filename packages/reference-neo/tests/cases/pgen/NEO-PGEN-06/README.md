# NEO-PGEN-06 — the 5 html-interactive probes toggle natively while hover and dark arms paint

The world renders one probe per html-interactive family member with
native wiring: an area inside its map, a details with summary and content,
a closed dialog, and live plus twin forms carrying the `_hover` arm. A
dark island details repaints a `_dark` summary override to prove the dark
arm beside a light control. Every probe paints through its style props.

styling-key absence, then proves native behavior: the details toggles open
and shut under clicks, the dialog stays closed, area and form attrs land,
the live form rests, hovers, and releases while its twin paints without
interaction, and the island summary repaints paper against the brand
control. Related: NEO-PRIM-03 (the hover precedent), NEO-PRIM-07 (the
island precedent), NEO-PGEN-03 (the form slice).
